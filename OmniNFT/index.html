OmniNFT: Modality-wise Diffusion Reinforcement for Joint Audio-Video Generation

Guohui Zhang1, Hang Xu1, Yuming Li2, Siming Fu3, Zeyue Xue3, Jie Huang3, Hu Yu1,
Lin Song3, Feng Zhao1†
1University of Science and Technology of China, 2Peking University, 3JD Explore Academy
†Corresponding author
Paper (ArXiv) Code

Abstract

Reinforcement Learning (RL) offers a promising paradigm for aligning joint audio-video generation models with complex, multi-objective goals, yet its effective extension to this setting remains underexplored. We observe that mainstream dual-stream audio-visual models exhibit a distinct misalignment between video and audio rewards: directly aggregating all reward signals into a unified advantage often induces advantage conflicts, resulting in suboptimal optimization. To address this, we propose OmniNFT, a modality-wise diffusion RL framework that computes independent, normalized advantages for each reward and selectively routes them by modality — video- and audio-specific advantages are directed to their corresponding branches, while cross-modal synchronization advantages are broadcast to both. Extensive experiments on JavisBench with the LTX-2 backbone demonstrate that OmniNFT achieves comprehensive improvements in audio quality, visual quality, cross-modal alignment, and audio-visual synchronization over strong baselines.



Motivation: Advantage Conflict

Mainstream audio-visual foundation models adopt a dual-stream architecture with cross-modal interaction. Directly aggregating all reward signals into a unified advantage (GRPO) and optimizing both branches simultaneously often leads to misalignment. We analyze 1,400 generated samples and find that nearly half exhibit advantage conflicts — where video and audio rewards point in opposite directions. This indicates that direct reward aggregation leads to advantage dilution, hindering effective optimization.

Figure 1: Advantage conflict between audio and video modality. Nearly half of the samples show conflicting advantage directions.



Core Idea

OmniNFT focuses on a simple but effective principle: separate reward credit by modality. Instead of forcing audio and video branches to follow one mixed RL signal, we compute reward-wise normalized advantages and route them to the right branch.

  • Video rewards only optimize the video branch.
  • Audio rewards only optimize the audio branch.
  • Sync rewards are shared by both branches to improve AV alignment.
